# Governance Model Draft 0.1

## Proposed bodies
- **Technical Council:** specifications, compatibility and release standards.
- **Public-interest Assembly:** users, educators, researchers, civil society, implementers and affected communities.
- **Independent Audit:** security, privacy and governance integrity.
- **Stewardship entity:** assets and maintenance under a public-benefit mandate, subject to legal review.

No body controls code, standards, user data and governance unilaterally. Funding does not confer protocol control. Technical maintainers do not gain access to personal data by role.

## Change process
Public RFC → impact assessment → technical and independent review → public comment → reasoned decision with dissent record → versioned release/migration → post-release evaluation.

Emergency security changes are scoped, documented, reviewed retrospectively and reversible where possible.

## Anti-capture safeguards
Transparent funding/conflicts; rotation; public records with privacy exceptions; multiple maintainers; no exclusive vendor rights; appeals and dispute resolution.

## Legal note
“Owned by humanity” is an ethical goal, not a self-executing legal status. Entity form, fiduciary duties, licensing, trademarks and jurisdiction require qualified legal advice and broad consultation.
